# Exec test: "Gen Meeting" (Monday, Oct 5, 2026)

A practice run of the whole system with real people and real emails. Nothing here is a real event. Takes about 20 minutes.

## Before (Gordon, 10 minutes)
1. Exec Portal → Events → **New event**. Name: **Gen Meeting**. Date: Oct 5. Add two ticket types: **Free** ($0) and **Paid** ($2). Add one question, for example "Favourite snack".
2. Set it to **Published**. The link is on the event's row (events.ucalgarycss.ca).
3. Send the link to the execs who are testing.

## Everyone (each person, use your real email)
1. Open the event link on your phone. Register **one person** with the **Free** ticket.
2. Register again with the **Paid** ticket, and add one friend or your own second name as a second person.
3. Check your inbox (and spam). You should get a "registered" email for each. The paid one says how much to send and the code to put in the e-transfer message.
4. Open **events.ucalgarycss.ca → My tickets**. Put in your email. You should get an email with your tickets.
5. Open **member.ucalgarycss.ca**. Try **I forgot which email I used** with your UCID and last name. You should land in your pass. Press **My tickets**: your tickets should show and the pass should go away. Press **Back to my pass**.

## Finance (Kevin or whoever has the e-transfer)
1. Payments tab → pick Gen Meeting. You should see the paid orders waiting.
2. Mark one order **paid**. That person should get a ticket email with a QR code within a minute.
3. Leave one order unpaid on purpose (we need it for the door).

## Door (two people, phones)
1. Go to **exec.ucalgarycss.ca/scanner/**.
2. One person signs in with the **exec password** and opens entry: Door tab → tap the entry button so it says **Entry open**.
3. The other person signs in with **Door sign-in (name only)** and just types their name.
4. Scan tickets: a paid ticket from each person's phone. Try to break it:
   - Scan the same ticket twice (should say already checked in).
   - Scan the unpaid person's ticket (should say not paid, go to help desk).
   - Scan a screenshot of a ticket (should work).
   - Scan the member pass QR (should say it's a membership card, not a ticket).
5. Close entry. The door-only person should be kicked out with "Door scanning is closed".

## After
- Payments → **Email everyone** → "Only people who have paid" → send a short thank-you. Check it arrives.
- Event → **Attendees** → **Download list (CSV)**. Open it.
- Settings → **Run health check** (admin). Everything should say OK.

## What to tell Gordon
Write down anything confusing, slow, or wrong. Include: what you tapped, what you expected, what happened, and a screenshot. Wording that sounds odd counts too.
