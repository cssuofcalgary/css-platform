# Exec portal guide — working draft

Notes moved out of the interface during the copy cleanup. This is a starting point for a future guide, not a finished handbook. Review the wording and steps against the final screens before sharing.

The existing [handbook](../HANDBOOK.md) covers the wider workflow.

## Waitlist

Use **Add waitlist** to let people queue when an event fills up.

## Reference notes from the interface

### Capacity

Leave capacity blank for no limit. Choose whether capacity counts paid tickets only or all active registrations.

### Member tickets

“Members only” checks the member ID or UCID. Tickets that cannot be verified need a help-desk check.

### ov Sub

Here's what's happening with CSS.

### members Sub

Look anyone up by name, email, UCID or member ID.

### pending Note

Sign-ups from the website and Add member land here. Older members who aren't marked paid are under Search.

### partners Hint

Members see the ones that are On on their pass page. Switch a partner Off to hide it without deleting it.

### redeem Hint

Every time a member shows a partner deal on their pass, it is logged here (members opening their pass from an emailed link).

### Confirming membership payment

Check the e-transfer or the cash first. Marking paid emails them their member pass.

### Member sign-ups

Sign-ups from the website land here. Check the payment, then tap Mark paid.

### events Sub

Create, publish and run every CSS event from here.

### ed Basics Hint

What people see first on the event page.

### ed Schedule Hint

Times are Mountain Time.

### ed Capacity Hint

Decide when an event counts as full.

### Waitlist

People on the waitlist get no ticket and take no spot. When a spot opens, the VP Finance offers it from the Payments tab (Waitlist). Nothing moves by itself.

### Event email choices

Each email uses part of the club's daily sending limit, so only switch on what you need. Whatever you choose here, the page people see after signing up always shows their ticket link, and on a paid event how much to send, where, and their payment code.

### Registration confirmations

One email per registration, with the payment details. A free event never sends this: it emails the ticket instead.

### Ticket emails

On a free event the ticket is emailed as soon as someone registers. If this is off, nobody is emailed a ticket: people open it from the link on their sign-up page, and Finance can still press Resend tickets on an order.

### Payment reminders

Reminders are never sent by themselves. This only switches on the Send reminders button on the Payments tab for this event.

### ed Tickets Hint

Free tickets still get a QR code.

### ev Questions Hint

Name, email and UCID are always asked. Add anything else here.

### ev Closes Hint

Leave blank to close automatically when the event starts.

### ed Publishing Hint

Short letters at the start of every payment code (people put it in their e-transfer message).

### ev Code Prefix Hint

Starts every payment code for this event, e.g. MGN-4821. Leave blank to use the event's initials.

### pay Sub

Match e-transfers to orders, mark them paid, and email people.

### door Sub

Run the front table: open entry, add walk-ins, find people. Scanning happens on the scanner phones.

### act Sub

Everything that happens in the portal, newest first.

### set Etransfer Hint

Shown on the payment screen and in emails. Change it only if CSS gets a new e-transfer address.

### set Membership Hint

Where the system looks up members. Each year there's a new sheet: paste its link here. It is read before saving, so a wrong link can't break anything.

### set Pw Hint

At least 8 characters, and the two passwords must differ. Leave a pair blank to keep it as it is. Never write passwords in a document or a chat.

### em Hint

Change the words in the emails people get. The look stays the same. A blank box uses the standard wording.

### em Format Hint

A blank line starts a new paragraph. **Two stars** around words make them bold. Type just a dash (-) to show nothing in that spot.

### set Online Hint

Portals and scanner phones seen in the last 45 seconds. A locked or closed phone drops off.

### set Sessions Hint

Use this if a phone or laptop was left signed in. You stay signed in.

### set Health Hint

Checks the data sheet, membership sheet, backup, archive and email allowance. Every run is written to Activity.

### inv Refund Hint

Pre-filled with what these tickets cost. Enter less to keep a fee, or 0 for no refund.

### mail Explain

Google sets the limit and counts it over a rolling day, not at a fixed time. When it is used up, sign-ups and payments still work. Tickets whose email couldn't be sent wait here, and every ticket also opens from its own link. When Google allows more, press Send waiting emails.

### scan Log Hint

The last scans for this event, newest first: what the scanner read and what the system answered. Kept for a few hours.

### add Member Hint

For someone signing up at the table, or paid before the form existed. They get their member pass by email once it's marked paid.

### ma Paid Hint

Leave ticked if you have the money. Untick to add them as unpaid and mark them paid later.

### partner Offer Hint

For example: 10% OFF, or a free drink.

### em Paid Hint

Switching Unpaid to Paid emails the member their pass.

### mail Hint

Each person gets their own copy, in the usual look. Use Preview to see it first. The same address only gets one email.

### mail Fills

You can use {name} {event} {when} {where}. A blank line starts a new paragraph.

### add Hint

For someone who paid (e-transfer or cash) without registering online. They're added as paid and emailed their ticket.

### add Email Hint

Needed to send their ticket.

### edit Email Hint

Their ticket is sent again if this changes.

### edit Member Hint

Changing the member ID, UCID or ticket type checks the Membership sheet again.

### edit Flag Hint

Clear this to remove the warning from the door. Leave as is for the usual check.

### sec Exec Sub

Used by every exec to sign in

### sec Door Sub

Volunteers sign in with just a name, or enter this password if set

### sec Lock Sub

Then wait 10 minutes

### Recording a refund

Return the money by e-transfer or cash first, then record it here.

### duplicate Hint

Copied from another event. Pick the new date and check the details before saving.

### search Hint

Type at least 2 characters.

### bulk Hint

Tick orders to mark several paid at once.

### ov Overdue Note

The payment window passed. Close them to free up spots.

### ov Unsent Note

Open Payments and press Send now.

### ov Refunds Note

Return the money, then record it on the Payments tab.

### ov Part Paid Note

Waiting for the rest of the money. Open the Payments tab to see how much.

### ov Waitlist Note

Open Payments → Waitlist to offer a spot.

### ov Cancel Req Note

Open Payments to cancel them or keep them.

### ov Members Waiting Note

Check the e-transfer or cash, then mark them paid.
